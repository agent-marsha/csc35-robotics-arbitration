Arbitration · robot control architectures

Two behaviors want the wheels. What decides?

Every architecture answers the same question — only the rule differs.

01 · subsumption
Fixed priority

A higher layer suppresses a lower one. The winner is set before runtime; the loser is inhibited on the bus, not blended.

02 · motor schemas
Vector sum

Both behaviors keep talking every tick. The wheels receive their sum, so conflict is averaged into one blended command.

03 · behavior trees
Explicit threshold

The rule lives in the tree as data — how many children must succeed. Arbitration becomes a value you can edit, not buried wiring.

the point

Arbitration is the parallelism story: wear it as priority, summation, or an explicit policy — and you have answered who gets the actuators.
